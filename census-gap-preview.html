How to read this

Darker tracts indicate higher suspected need for the selected Census metric. Organization counts are based on visible mapped organizations within the selected radius from the tract center.

This does not measure eligibility, capacity, waitlists, hours, transportation routes, stigma, awareness, or whether countywide service is truly accessible. That is where partner interviews come in.
