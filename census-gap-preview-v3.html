How to read this

Darker tracts indicate higher suspected need for the selected Census metric. Red points are mapped organizations that match the current service lens and optional org type filter.

Priority signals are tracts that meet the selected need or asset-scarcity rule and have limited nearby matching assets inside the selected radius. Score cutoffs now use the relative 0 to 100 need score, not the raw Census percentage. Click a Priority Signal card to zoom to that tract.
